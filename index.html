Refund Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

We stand behind the quality of our design work. This Refund Policy outlines the circumstances under which refunds may be issued for our services and digital products.

1. Design on Demand Subscriptions

You may cancel your subscription at any time. Refunds are issued for the unused portion of the current billing cycle if requested within 7 days of the most recent charge. If you are unsatisfied with a specific deliverable, we will revise it at no additional cost - refunds for completed design work are evaluated on a case-by-case basis.

2. Custom Scope Engagements

Refund terms for fixed-fee projects are defined in the project SOW. Generally, the upfront deposit is non-refundable once work has commenced. If DesignGrids fails to deliver against the agreed-upon scope, you may be entitled to a partial or full refund of fees paid, proportional to the undelivered portion of work.

3. Digital Products (Figma Kits & Resources)

Due to the digital nature of our design resources, all sales of downloadable products (UI kits, design systems, icon sets, templates) are final. If you encounter a technical issue with a file - corruption, missing components, or broken auto-layout - contact us within 14 days and we will provide a corrected file or a full refund.

4. How to Request a Refund

Email billing@designgrids.studio with your order or invoice reference and a brief explanation. We review all requests within 3 business days and respond with a determination. Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days to the original payment method.

5. Chargebacks

We ask that you contact us before initiating a chargeback with your bank or payment provider. Most issues can be resolved quickly through direct communication - we're committed to making things right.
